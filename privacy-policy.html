We Save Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the We Save app handles information when you create an account and use its savings, expense, income and budgeting features.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, Firebase user identifier, and authentication provider information needed to sign you in.
  • Financial and budgeting information you enter: transaction amounts, income/expense type, category, dates, budget amounts, descriptions and names you choose to enter.
  • Authentication information: when you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, those providers and Firebase Authentication process the information needed to authenticate your account.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to provide account access, synchronize your We Save data across supported devices, display balances and budgets, and operate the features you request. We do not use your We Save transaction or budget information for advertising.

Optional Features

  • Export data: when you choose Export data (CSV), a file with your transactions is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (for example email or cloud storage). We do not receive a copy.
  • Daily reminder: if you turn this on, the reminder is scheduled on your device as a local notification. No information is sent to us or to any third party for this feature.
  • Language, theme, currency and reminder settings: these preferences are stored on your device only.

Service Providers

We Save uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide sign-in and cloud data storage. If you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, the selected provider is used to authenticate you. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Security

Data transmitted between We Save and Firebase is sent using encrypted network connections. Access to account data is tied to the authenticated Firebase user account. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is retained while your We Save account remains active. You can delete your account from inside the We Save app. You can also use the external account-deletion page below. The deletion process removes the We Save Firebase Authentication account and the Firestore document associated with that account, including saved transactions and budgets.

Delete your We Save account

Children

We Save is not designed specifically for children. If the app is later offered to children, the developer will update the app and this policy as required by applicable law and Google Play policies.

Privacy Questions

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the We Save Google Play listing. This policy applies specifically to the We Save app.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when We Save features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
